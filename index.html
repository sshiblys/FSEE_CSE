New Facility · Opening December 2025

Inside the new GMIoT building

A first look at the Greater Manchester Institute of Technology — a £14.3m, low-carbon building taking shape right next to the SEE Building on Peel Park campus.

Designed by jmarchitects and built by Tilbury Douglas, the two-storey, 1,840m² GMIoT building will house digital labs, virtual reality spaces, a prototyping workshop and ICT studios — delivering Higher Technical Qualifications, HNDs and apprenticeships across construction, engineering, digital, media and health science, in partnership with GCHQ, Siemens, Laing O’Rourke, TalkTalk and the BBC.

The building is A-rated and low carbon, featuring a green roof with photovoltaic panels and a living wall supported by rainwater harvesting — part of the University’s commitment to Net Zero Carbon by 2038. Its exterior shingle cladding was deliberately colour-matched: green tiles echoing neighbouring Peel Park, and red tiles to match the SEE Building next door.
